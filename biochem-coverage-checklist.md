# Biochemistry & Genetics — Module-Level Coverage Checklist

This is the **source of truth for completeness** for the biochem guide. Each unchecked box
is one module to write (one `### Topic` heading, Pompe-level granularity). We verify the
finished guide against this list, not against any single textbook.

**How to read it.** Discrete diseases get their own module (boards test them individually);
pathways get a module each with regulation folded in. A `[+table]` tag means the section
also gets a buzzword/differential comparison table. Notes in *italics* flag judgment calls
for your review.

**Cross-subject boundaries (resolved):**
- Molecular genetics (DNA/RNA/replication/translation) stays here, per convention.
- Cytoskeleton/motor proteins and vesicular trafficking *mechanisms* are **canonical in
  Cell & Molecular Biology**; biochem carries one-line **stubs** (shown below). I-cell
  *disease* stays as a full module here in Lysosomal Storage Diseases (it's a metabolic enzyme-targeting disorder),
  cross-referencing the trafficking mechanism.
- Enzyme defects with a pathology home (gout, hemolysis) are written here for the
  biochemical mechanism and cross-referenced to ROBBINS.
